Description

  • 【Comprehensive Protection】: This 7-piece accessory set is specially designed for the new generation Meta Quest 3, providing dustproof, shockproof and scratchproof protection. This accessory is washable and reusable, with a soft and lightweight texture, providing effective protection for your VR device.
  • 【VR Protective Cover Set includes】: 1 * Host Cover, 1 * Face Mask, 2 * Grip Handle Covers, 2 * Button Caps, 1 * Lens Cover, 1 * Eye Mask, and 1 * Cleaning Cloth. Please note that the VR headset, controller and other accessories are not included.
  • 【TPU Host Cover Protection】: Made of high quality TPU material, this host cover provides drop and slip resistance. It does not need to be removed, allowing seamless charging and use of basic functions. With its four-sided breathability, it provides optimal heat dissipation and maintains the performance of your VR headset while gaming.
  • 【TPU Grip Grips】: These grip covers are made of high-quality TPU and feature non-slip textures for an aesthetic and comfortable grip. Thanks to the precise openings, there is no delay in using the grip covers. Also, two silicone caps for the buttons are included to reduce finger pressure when pressing the buttons frequently, delaying fatigue and improving the gaming experience.
  • 【TPU Face Mask and Sweatproof Eye Mask】: Made of high quality TPU, this face mask offers a soft and smooth texture to cushion the pressure on the facial skin. It is non-irritating and reusable.Eye mask made of elastic fabric, comfortable to wear, moisture-wicking fabric keeps face and ears dry.
